# URT — Your Service Tracker

## Introduction

URT is a web-based service operations platform for independent electronics repair shops. It brings the shop owner, technicians, and customers into one shared repair workflow, from device intake and diagnosis to estimate approval, repair progress, and pickup.

Instead of coordinating every ticket through separate paper notes and private chats, a shop can use URT to keep service records and work status together. Customers can follow a ticket online, while the shop team works from role-specific views.

![An electronics repair shop owner, technician, and customer connected through a shared service workflow.](assets/urt-saas/01-introduction.png)

**For:** laptop, smartphone, computer, and electronics repair businesses that want a more organized customer and technician workflow.

## The problem

Repair work often crosses several people and communication channels. When the details live in paper receipts, personal messages, and verbal updates, it becomes harder to:

- Find a device’s intake notes and previous service history.
- See which technician owns each job and what is waiting in the queue.
- Keep diagnosis, price estimates, and customer approval aligned.
- Answer repeated “what is the status?” questions while the team is repairing devices.
- Hand a completed device back with a clear record of the work.

Scattered information can lead to delays, missed updates, and disagreements about what was approved. URT gives each repair a trackable record and keeps the people involved connected to the same service journey.

![A repair shop owner overwhelmed by disconnected paper records, messages, and unclear assignments.](assets/urt-saas/02-problem.png)

## Use case: a repair from intake to pickup

Consider a customer bringing a laptop with a charging problem to a repair shop:

1. **The owner records the intake.** The shop creates a service ticket with the device and reported issue, then assigns it to a technician.
2. **The technician investigates.** The technician reviews the assigned work, records a diagnosis, and prepares an estimate for the required repair.
3. **The customer makes an informed decision.** The customer can review the estimate and approve or decline it through the customer flow. The ticket keeps the decision with the service record.
4. **The team completes the repair.** The technician updates the job as work progresses and completes the shop’s quality check before marking it ready for pickup.
5. **The customer follows progress.** The customer can use the service ticket to check progress online, reducing the need to contact the shop for every update.

The owner can oversee tickets, customers, technicians, and operational reports. The technician gets an assigned work queue and repair actions. The customer gets a view of their service request, estimate decision, and ticket progress.
